# Impact of Free Transport Facility for Women on Labour Force Participation in India

## Overview

This project evaluates whether fare-free public transport policies increase female labour force participation (FLFP) in India by reducing mobility constraints and commuting costs.

Several Indian states, including Delhi, Karnataka, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana, have implemented free or subsidized bus travel schemes for women. This study examines whether these policies translate into higher labour market participation.

## Research Question

Do fare-free bus transport schemes increase female labour force participation in India?

## Data

* CMIE Consumer Pyramids Household Survey (CPHS)
* Approximately 170,000 households
* Longitudinal panel covering 2014–2024
* Women aged 16–60

## Methodology

* Staggered Difference-in-Differences (DiD)
* Callaway & Sant'Anna (2021) estimator
* State Fixed Effects
* Time Fixed Effects
* Event Study Analysis
* Heterogeneity Analysis

## Key Findings

* Free transport policies generated meaningful welfare gains through reduced commuting costs.
* Female mobility and public transport usage increased.
* However, female labour force participation did not experience a significant increase.
* Structural barriers such as unpaid care responsibilities, labour demand constraints, and social norms continue to limit participation.
